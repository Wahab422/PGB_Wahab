Frequently asked questions
and the drawings to go with your set

How wide can Melius doors be?

Melius door widths depend on the door type, height, glass specification and hardware. Larger doors may require a custom solution. Confirm your opening dimensions early so we can review sizing, operating weight and hardware requirements.

Can Melius accommodate uneven floors?

Melius can accommodate variations in floor level through its glass sizing and framing details. The floor within a door’s swing or sliding path needs particular attention to maintain clearance and proper operation. Site measurements determine any adjustments required before fabrication.

Can Melius accommodate custom door hardware?

Yes. Melius can accommodate custom door hardware. We may need to review your selected hardware with our engineer to confirm compatibility with the door and frame. Electric strikes, access control and room-booking systems should also be coordinated before fabrication.

How can Melius meet our acoustic performance requirements?

Melius offers single- and double-glazed configurations for different acoustic requirements. Performance depends on the glass, door assembly, seals and surrounding construction. Confirm your acoustic target early so the complete assembly can be specified appropriately.

What blocking is required for Melius?

Melius requires solid backing at its head and jamb attachment points. When installed underneath a T-bar or drop ceiling, blocking is required above the ceiling tiles. Blocking and bracing details should be coordinated for the ceiling construction and site conditions.
