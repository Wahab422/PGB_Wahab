Origin Story

Founded in 2015 by Ben Solomon after completing the National Science Foundation’s I-Corps program, FedTech is a Deep Tech commercialization partner, not a vendor. We design and run programs for federal agencies, national labs, and corporations, and stay with the companies we build through to fielded use.

We partner with U.S. federal agencies and laboratories, research institutions, universities, and private-sector organizations to identify high-potential innovations and accelerate their path to market.

Our Mission

We don't take equity. We don't sell software. We're a fully integrated, sponsor-aligned neutral operator whose success is measured by the outcomes of the programs we run.

Despite significant annual investment in breakthrough research, translating those discoveries into fielded products remains complex. We address this through structured programs designed to:

Identify high-potential, disruptive technologies
Match innovations with entrepreneurs and industry partners
Accelerate progression from concept or patent to market-ready solutions
Equip participants with mentorship, funding strategy, business validation, and investor readiness
