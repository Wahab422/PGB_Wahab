Daniel’s story

Daniel Lubetzky is an entrepreneur, investor, and civic leader committed to replacing 'Us vs. Them' with Solving Problems Together.

The son of a Holocaust survivor, Daniel has spent his life building bridges - first by promoting economic cooperation in the Middle East, then by building the OneVoice Movement, the largest grassroots movement of Israelis and Palestinians working toward peaceful coexistence.

‍

Daniel launched the iconic KIND brand from his basement in 2004 and grew it into a multibillion-dollar brand known for health transparency and purpose-driven business.

‍

Today, through Camino Partners, Daniel backs enduring businesses focused on enhancing consumers’ lives. With Builders, he helps citizens move beyond “us vs. them” mindsets to find common ground and build on it together. A political independent, he brings a values-driven lens to everything from entrepreneurship to civic life.

‍

A New York Times bestselling author and now full-time Shark on Shark Tank, Daniel’s work has been recognized with honors including The King Center’s Beloved Community Award, the Ellis Island Medal of Honor, and the Muhammad Ali Humanitarian Award. President Obama named him a Presidential Ambassador for Global Entrepreneurship.

‍

Born in Mexico, Daniel came to the U.S. at 16, and earned a BA from Trinity and a JD from Stanford. He enjoys spending time with his wife and four children, and loves practicing magic.

HOW IT STARTED

HOW IT’s going

1968

Daniel Is Born

Daniel Lubetzky was born in Mexico City in 1968.

1984

Immigrates to San Antonio

1990

Earns B.A. from Trinity University

Receives a B.A. in Economics and International Relations from Trinity University.

1993

Graduates Stanford Law School

1994

Starts PeaceWorks

Starts PeaceWorks, which encourages economic cooperation between neighbors in conflict regions through the creation of delicious foods.

2002

ONEVOICE

Creates OneVoice, a grassroots organization that amplifies the voices of mainstream Israelis and Palestinians to become more vocal advocates for a two-state solution.

2004

LAUNCHES kind

Launches KIND, creating a new healthy snack category with a commitment to honoring his father’s legacy.

2015

do the kind thing published

Launches a New York Times bestselling book Do the KIND Thing.

2017

Empatico & Mars partnership

Launches the $20M Empatico initiative to connect students globally through virtual exchanges.

Partners with Mars Inc. to expand global access to KIND while remaining majority owner and CEO.

2018

Lubetzky Family Foundation

Launches the Lubetzky Family Foundation to combat antisemitism and support Jewish communities.

2019

joins shark tank

Makes his Shark Tank debut as a Guest Shark before becoming a Regular Shark in 2024.

2020

frontline impact project

Launched Frontline Impact Project through The KIND Foundation to support frontline workers.

Sold a controlling stake of KIND to Mars Inc.

2023

Camino partners

Establishes Camino Partners, a privately-held investment platform focused on enhancing consumers’ lives.

2024

Builders movement

Launches Builders Movement alongside over 300 leaders to equip citizens to replace “us vs. them” with solving problems together.

Sells remaining KIND stake to Mars.

2026

build or break podcast

Launched Build or Break, a podcast where Daniel goes deep with the world’s most fearless builders.

What Daniel’s Building

KIND snacks & CAMINO Partners

Daniel launched the iconic KIND brand from his basement in 2004 and grew it into a multibillion-dollar brand known for health transparency and purpose-driven business. Today, through Camino Partners, Daniel backs enduring businesses focused on enhancing consumers’ lives.

shark tank

Known as ‘The Kind Shark’ and the only Hispanic Shark on the show, Daniel is a regular panelist on ABC’s Emmy Award-winning series, Shark Tank, where he supports entrepreneurs in building toward the American Dream.

Social Impact

The son of a Holocaust survivor, Daniel’s social impact work focuses on combatting antisemitism and all forms of hate. Daniel founded the Builders Movement with over 300 leaders to replace a culture of ‘us versus them’ with bridging divides to build durable solutions together.
